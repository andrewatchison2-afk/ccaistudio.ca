Questions
Is this really personalised for my player?
Yes. Every plan is built from your player's position, age, and level — not a generic template. A 10-year-old winger gets completely different advice from a 14-year-old defenceman. We use Claude (the AI behind it) to reason about what your specific player needs, not what players in general need.
What ages and levels does this work for?
Ages 6–18, all positions, from House League through Rep AAA. The AI adjusts everything — exercise types, intensity, complexity, and coaching language — based on age and level. A House League Novice player gets age-appropriate fun fundamentals. A Rep AAA Bantam player gets elite development details.
