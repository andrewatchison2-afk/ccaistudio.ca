Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Coachd Hockey ("Coachd", "we", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of parents and players who use our service. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What We Collect

When you sign up, we collect:

  • Parent name and email address
  • Player name, age, position, level, and gender
  • Development goals and optional tryout date
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe; we never store card details
  • Chat messages between you and the AI coach

How We Use It

  • To generate personalised weekly hockey development plans for your player
  • To power the AI coach chat, which uses your player's profile and conversation history to give relevant advice
  • To process and manage your subscription via Stripe
  • To contact you about your account (we do not send marketing emails without your consent)

Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — Player profile data and chat messages are sent to Anthropic's API to generate plans and coaching responses. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to data processed through their API.
  • Stripe — Handles all payment processing. We share your email and name with Stripe for billing purposes. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.
  • Railway — Our hosting provider. Your data is stored on Railway's infrastructure in accordance with their privacy policy.

Data Retention

We retain your player profile, plans, and chat history for as long as your account is active. If you cancel and request deletion, we will remove your data within 30 days. Email us at andy@coachdhockey.ca to request deletion.

Children's Privacy

Coachd is intended for use by parents on behalf of minor hockey players. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13. Accounts must be created and managed by a parent or guardian.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at andy@coachdhockey.ca.

Email Communications (CASL)

We send transactional emails only — account confirmations, billing receipts, and service notices related to your subscription. We do not send marketing or promotional emails without your explicit consent.

If you receive any email from us you did not expect, you can opt out at any time by emailing andy@coachdhockey.ca with "Unsubscribe" in the subject line. We will remove you within 10 business days as required under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email andy@coachdhockey.ca.
